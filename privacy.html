Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: your appointments, contacts, notes and everything else you put in MyServiceTime are encrypted on your own device with your password before they go online. We cannot read them. We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.

1. Who we are

MyServiceTime is run by ("we", "us"). For any question about your privacy, write to .

2. What we keep, and why

  • Your account: your email address, the name you type when you sign up, and when the account was created. We use these to let you sign in, and send you sign-in and password-reset emails.
  • Feedback you send: if you use “Send feedback”, your rating, message, the optional email you give for a reply, and technical facts that help fix problems (app version, language, type of device and browser) are sent to us, not encrypted. We only use them to improve MyServiceTime.
  • How to reach you: your phone number and contact email (you give them in “About you”). They are kept with your account, not encrypted, so we can tell you if MyServiceTime has a problem — for example when the app is down — and about important changes to the service. We don't use them for anything else, and we never share or sell them.
  • Your MyServiceTime data: appointments, visits, notes, contacts (including any photos you add), assignments, planner items, events, time tracker sessions and your profile picture. These are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your password) and stored only in that encrypted form. We cannot open them, and neither can our hosting provider.
  • Your subscription: where you bought it (our website through Stripe, Google Play or the App Store), your Stripe customer number if you paid on the website, whether your subscription is active, when it renews, and the currency you pay in. Your card details go directly to Stripe, Google or Apple; we never see or store them.
  • Reminder notifications (only if you turn them on): the times your upcoming reminders are due — not what they are about — plus the notification address of each device you allowed and the language for the alert text. The alert itself only says that you have a reminder.
  • On your device: the app keeps an encrypted copy of your data and your settings (like your language) in your browser's storage so it works quickly and offline.
  • Moments (for Supporters): the photos you save in Moments are made smaller and encrypted, and stay on that device only; they are not uploaded to the Cloud.
  • Backup file (free, only if you make one): “Back up my data” saves a copy of your data in a file on your device. That file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. We never receive it.
  • Territories: your territory cards (numbers, dates, notes, map links, pins and outlines) are encrypted with the rest of your data. A photo of a territory card stays encrypted on that device only. Scanning a card's QR code happens on your device; the picture is not sent anywhere.
  • App lock (only if you turn it on): your passcode is never stored, only a scrambled check of it, on that device only. Face ID and fingerprints are checked by your own phone or computer; we never receive them.

3. Information about other people

If you add contacts or assign tasks to someone, that information is encrypted with the rest of your data and only you can see it. Please only add information you have a right to keep. When you use "send" for an assignment, it opens your own email or text-message app; MyServiceTime doesn't send it for you.

4. Services we rely on

  • Supabase — hosts accounts and the encrypted data (servers in the United States).
  • Stripe — handles payments and receipts on our website, under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google Play and the App Store — handle payments made inside the phone apps (outside the United States), under Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat — checks Google Play and App Store purchases and tells us whether your Supporter plan is active. It receives your account's ID number (not your name, email or any of your data) and the purchase details, under RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Your phone or browser's notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) — delivers reminder alerts if you turn them on.
  • Address search (OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon) — receives the address text you search for, not who you are.
  • Exchange rates (open.er-api.com) — used to show prices in your currency; it receives no personal information.
  • Maps — the Map in “My tools” shows map pictures from OpenStreetMap, which sees the area you are looking at (not your contacts or notes). When you tap an address, it opens in a map app or website you choose, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Your location — only if you tap “Near me” or the 📍 button. It stays on your device and in your encrypted data; it is used to sort places by distance and suggest nearby addresses.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo) — only if you turn the weather on. It receives your location rounded to about 1 km (never your name, email or data) and sends back the forecast. For an appointment or event far from you that has a place on the map, it also receives that place's area, rounded to about 10 km, to show the weather there. The forecast stays on your device and is not uploaded; you can turn the weather off any time in Settings.

Like any website, these services can see your device's internet (IP) address when your device connects to them.

5. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • We never show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We never read your encrypted data, and we can't.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu (Delete account). This cancels your subscription and permanently removes your account, your encrypted data, your reminder times and your devices from our servers. Stripe may keep payment records for as long as the law requires.

7. Your password

Your password is what unlocks your data, and we never receive it in a readable form. If you forget it, you can reset it from a device where you're still signed in. If you have no such device, your old data can't be recovered — by you or by us — and you can start over with an empty account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, download or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else write to and we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Children

MyServiceTime is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.

This page is in English. If it's translated, the English version is the one that applies.
